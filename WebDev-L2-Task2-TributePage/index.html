Biography

From a village pitch to the world stage

Mohamed Salah is an Egyptian footballer whose journey from the village of Nagrig to the highest levels of world football has made him one of Egypt's most celebrated sporting figures. From a young age, he showed extraordinary dedication to football, traveling long distances for training while continuing his education.

Salah began his senior career with Al Mokawloon Al Arab before moving to Europe. At the age of 20, he joined Swiss club FC Basel, where he won two Swiss league titles and attracted international attention through his performances in European competitions. His career then took him to Chelsea, Fiorentina and Roma, where he continued developing into an elite attacking player.

In 2017, Salah joined Liverpool and quickly established himself as one of the club's most influential players. His first season produced 44 goals, and his consistency over the following years helped Liverpool win major trophies including the UEFA Champions League, Premier League, FIFA Club World Cup and FA Cup. By 2026, Liverpool recorded 442 appearances and 257 goals for Salah during his nine-year spell at the club.

Salah's achievements also extend beyond his club career. With Egypt, he has become a symbol of determination and national pride, helping the country return to the FIFA World Cup in 2018 after a 28-year absence. His individual honours include four Premier League Golden Boots, and he became the first player to finish a Premier League season as the leading scorer and assist provider while also winning the Player of the Season award in 2024/25.

Career Timeline

Key milestones and turning points

  1. 1992

    Born in Nagrig, Egypt.

  2. 2010

    Made his Egyptian Premier League debut with Al Mokawloon Al Arab.

  3. 2012

    Moved to FC Basel and began his European career.

  4. 2014

    Joined Chelsea and later moved to Italian football.

  5. 2015

    Joined Roma permanently after impressing during his time in Italy.

  6. 2017

    Signed for Liverpool and began a historic chapter of his career.

  7. 2018

    Helped Egypt qualify for the FIFA World Cup for the first time since 1990.

  8. 2019

    Won the UEFA Champions League with Liverpool.

  9. 2020

    Won his first Premier League title with Liverpool.

  10. 2024/25

    Won the Premier League Golden Boot, Playmaker award and Player of the Season award.

  11. 2026

    Completed his nine-year Liverpool chapter with 257 goals in 442 appearances.
